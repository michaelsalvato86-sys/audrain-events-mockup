Privacy notice

This notice explains what the Concours Foundation collects when you buy tickets online or use the Audrain app. TODO counsel: full privacy policy, including state privacy laws that apply to the Foundation, retention periods, and children (COPPA).

What we collect

  • Order details: names of ticket holders, buyer name, email, items bought, amounts.
  • Payment: handled by Stripe; we receive the last four digits and card brand only.
  • Entry: when and where your pass was scanned.
  • Device key: the app and this site create a private key on your device that never leaves it; we store only its public half to prove your QR code is genuine.
  • Marketing attribution: a first-party visitor id, the ad or link that brought you here (UTM tags, ad click ids such as fbclid or gclid) and first-party advertising cookies (_fbp, _fbc).

Advertising and your choices

We may share purchase events with Meta, Google and TikTok to measure our advertising. Use the "Do not sell or share my info" link on the ticket page, or turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, and we stop setting advertising cookies and recording your activity on that device.
